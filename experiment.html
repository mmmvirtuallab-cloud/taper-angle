MEASUREMENT OF INTERNAL TAPER ANGLE

AIM:

To compare and assess the methods of internal taper measurement with aid of:

  • Vernier Calliper
  • Precision Spheres

APPARATUS REQUIRED:

  • Vernier Calliper
  • Precision Balls
  • Micrometer
  • Vernier Depth gauge
  • Specimen with a tapered hole

FORMULA USED:

sin θ = (R-r) / [ (h+r) - (H + R) ]

where,

  • θ = internal taper angle (theta)
  • R = radius of big precision ball (mm)
  • r = radius of small precision ball (mm)
  • H = depth of big precision ball from surface (mm)
  • h = depth of small precision ball from surface (mm)

PROCEDURE:

Vernier Caliper Method

  1. Length of the specimen is measured.
  2. Diameter of the of the big end and small end of the specimen is measured (internal diameter) using vernier caliper.
  3. Value θ is calculated using formula.
