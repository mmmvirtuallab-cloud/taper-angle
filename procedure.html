Procedure for Measuring Internal Taper Angle

1. Measure the diameter of both balls accurately using a vernier caliper.

2. Place the smaller ball inside the tapered hole.

3. Using the height gauge, measure the height of the ball from the reference surface (h1).

4. Remove the smaller ball and place the larger ball in the tapered hole.

5. Measure the height of the larger ball from the same reference surface (h2).

6. Calculate the difference in heights (h2 - h1).

7. Calculate the taper angle using the formula:

sin α = (R - r) / (h2 - h1)

where R = radius of larger ball, r = radius of smaller ball
